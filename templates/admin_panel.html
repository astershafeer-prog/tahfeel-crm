📶 Meta Conversions API (lead-quality feedback)
Sends a “good lead” signal back to Meta whenever a lead is marked Genuine, so your Lead Ads optimise for quality instead of just form-fills. Only Meta-sourced leads are sent (hashed phone/email + the Meta lead id — no plain personal data). Stays off until your marketing agency provides the details below and you enable it.
You can paste the whole endpoint URL from Meta's setup page (https://graph.facebook.com/…/events?access_token=…) — the token is taken out of it automatically.

💰 Meta ad spend sync (Campaign ROI)
Pulls what each campaign spent from Meta each night and fills it into Campaign ROI, so nobody copies numbers out of Ads Manager by hand. Read-only access — this cannot change your ads, budgets or spend. While it's on, synced campaigns become read-only in the report so a typed number can't be silently overwritten.
