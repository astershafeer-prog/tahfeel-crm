Revenue is cash basis — closed jobs plus part-payments received — the same rule the dashboard uses, counted for clients who came from this month's leads. Cost per client is this month's spend divided by clients won from this month's leads; early in a month it will look terrible, because the spend has landed but the deals haven't closed yet. Judge a month once it has had time to mature. Leads whose campaign name is missing are grouped under — no campaign name —; that means Meta didn't return one, not that they came from nowhere.

The ad rows under each campaign are the individual creatives. Their spend comes straight from Meta and can't be typed in, so a creative shows no cost until the sync has run for that month. Ad spend won't always add up to the campaign's total — Meta attributes some spend at campaign level only — so use the ad rows to compare creatives against each other, and the campaign row for the money. Leads that arrived before ad tracking existed sit under — unknown ad —; that's a gap in our records, not a mystery ad.
