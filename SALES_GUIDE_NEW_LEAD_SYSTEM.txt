THE NEW PIPELINE (Follow in order)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  ① NEW → ② CONTACTED → ③ QUALIFIED → ④ PROPOSAL → ⑤ CONVERTED
                                            ↓
                                    🚫 LOST (if no deal)
                                            ↓
                                    🔮 FUTURE (if parking)


WHAT EACH STAGE MEANS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

① NEW
   Fresh lead just arrived (Meta ad, website form, etc.)
   Action: Call/WhatsApp to qualify

② CONTACTED
   You've reached out and spoken to them (or tried and failed)
   Action: Log the activity (call, no answer, WhatsApp, etc.)

③ QUALIFIED
   You spoke to them. They have real interest.
   Action: Send quotation (move to PROPOSAL once sent)

④ PROPOSAL
   Quote delivered. Awaiting their feedback.
   Action: Follow up if silent. Move to CONVERTED when they say YES.

⑤ CONVERTED
   Deal won. You've signed them up.
   Action: Handover to operations/delivery team


SIDE EXITS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

🚫 LOST
   They said no, or you've given up (too many no-answers, competitor won, etc.)
   → Add a reason: "Price too high", "Went cold", "Chose competitor", etc.

🔮 FUTURE
   Not ready now, but revisit later (e.g., "Call in 3 months when budget opens")
   → Set a revisit date. System will remind you and auto-surface the lead.


WHAT TO LOG EACH TIME (Activities)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Activity = WHAT YOU DID. Pick one:

  📞 Call — connected          = You spoke to them (add notes)
  ☎️  Call — no answer         = They didn't pick up (bumps attempt counter)
  💬 WhatsApp sent             = You sent a WhatsApp message
  📧 Email sent                = You sent an email (quote, follow-up, etc.)
  💰 Quote sent                = You shared a quotation
  📅 Meeting                   = You scheduled or did a demo/call
  📝 Note                      = General update (no specific action)

⚠️  IMPORTANT: Logging an activity does NOT change the stage. You must
   manually select the stage. This keeps your pipeline clean.


LEAD QUALITY (Your Judgment)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

After you interact, mark the lead's QUALITY (click button on lead page):

  ✓ GENUINE        = They're interested & qualified. Keep pursuing.
  ✕ JUNK           = Wrong number / spam / not interested (add reason)
  ⊘ UNREACHABLE    = Never answers / gone silent (YOU decide when, manual only)

⚠️  MANUAL ONLY: Unreachable is NEVER automatic. YOU decide based on:
   - How many times you've tried
   - How long you've waited
   - Whether they respond to any channel


WORKFLOW EXAMPLE 1: Fresh Lead (First Contact)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Lead arrives (Status = NEW)
2. You call them
   ↓
3. Log Activity: "Call — connected" (or "no answer")
4. Change STAGE to "CONTACTED"
5. Write remark: "Spoke to owner, interested in VAT services"
6. Set follow-up date: Tomorrow (system auto-alerts you)
7. Mark QUALITY: "Genuine" (they seem real)
   OR "Junk" (spam/blocked) + reason
8. If they want a quote → move STAGE to "PROPOSAL"


WORKFLOW EXAMPLE 2: Silent Lead (Multiple Attempts)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Lead is in PROPOSAL (quote sent 5 days ago)
2. You call → no answer
   Log Activity: "Call — no answer"  [Attempt counter now = 1]
3. Day 3: You send WhatsApp reminder
   Log Activity: "WhatsApp sent"
4. Day 5: You call again → still no answer
   Log Activity: "Call — no answer"  [Attempt counter now = 2]
5. Day 7: You give up / decide they're unreachable
   Mark QUALITY: "UNREACHABLE" (click button) + reason: "No response"
   Change STAGE to "LOST"
6. Report shows: lead went cold after quote


KEY POINTS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

✓ STAGE = Where in the pipeline (New → Contacted → ... → Converted)
✓ ACTIVITY = What you just did (call, WhatsApp, email, etc.) — logged per update
✓ QUALITY = Your judgment (Genuine / Junk / Unreachable) — manual, click button
✓ ATTEMPTS = Auto-counts "no answer" calls (informational only)
✓ FOLLOW-UP DATE = System alerts you when due

⚠️  Stage does NOT auto-change based on activity or attempts.
   You always choose the stage.

⚠️  Unreachable is NEVER automatic.
   You mark it when you've decided to stop trying.

⚠️  History is kept.
   Each activity logged = visible in the lead's timeline.
   Other staff can see who called when and what happened.


TROUBLESHOOTING:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: I logged an activity but the stage didn't change. Why?
A: Activities and stages are separate. You must manually select the stage.

Q: The system auto-marked someone Unreachable. Can I fix it?
A: This shouldn't happen — Unreachable is manual only. If it did, contact admin.

Q: What if the customer is genuinely unavailable but will call us back?
A: Leave stage as QUALIFIED/PROPOSAL. Log activity "Note: Waiting for callback".
   Set follow-up date for when they said they'd call.

Q: Can I skip a stage (e.g., go straight from NEW to PROPOSAL)?
A: Yes, if they ask for a quote immediately. Just log it and move the stage.
